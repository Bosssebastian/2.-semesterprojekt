\subsection{Use of generative artificial intelligence}

In the creation of this project report, the help of generative artificial intelligence has been used. The uses include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Brainstorming and idea generation to explore and refine many aspects of the report.
    \item Structuring the report, including the organization of sections and their contents.

    \item Help in adapting the text to a more academic writing style.

The ideas, work, results, and conclusions presented in this report are those of the group members and are not attributable to the AI tools used.
